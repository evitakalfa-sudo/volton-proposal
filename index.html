Three machines

Every supplier runs three machines. Volton can stand on two of them, for now.

Every supplier in Greece sells the same electricity over the same wires at the same regulated network charges. What is left to compete on is three machines: how you buy power, how a reading becomes a bill, and why a customer stays.

1
The trading desk.The four big groups hedge with their own generation. Volton hedges in the market. Not this year's fight.
2
The billing engine.The one contact every customer gets every month, and no supplier has claimed it.
3
The customer relationship.Volton has the stores and the adviser network. The part that keeps a customer after the sale is still to be built.

The core streams.

Stream 1, The offering

Volton cannot be the cheapest. It can be the one that knows your needs.

Why would anyone choose Volton?Initiatives we recommend
Volton sells 23 tariffs today. Stream 1 cuts them to a short, named set for households and for business, one a salesperson can explain and a customer can choose from, and tests each product on a small segment before launch.

01
B2B as the profit engine. Sell to small business through Volton's smart energy adviser. A shop uses more than twice a household, a bakery ten to thirty times.* 12 of Volton's 23 tariffs are business products.*
02
Bundles and cross-selling. Gas today. Then whatever partners can add: connectivity, banking, more. Which bundles, and in what order, is decided in the 58:hours methodology.
03
One account. One person, every meter they hold: the home, the children's flat, the summer house, the shop, the phone lines. One account, one view, one relationship.

Stream 3, The advisers

Keep the channel. Change what it gets paid for.

What is Volton paying advisers to actually do?Initiatives we recommend
Most of your customers arrive through advisers, paid in full at signature. Stream 3 pays them as the customer stays, so keeping customers earns more than moving volume.

01
A portfolio approach. Understand each channel, segment the advisers, and tier them on the value of the customers they hold, not the volume they move.
02
Pilot first. Two or three adviser groups, measured, before the network moves.
03
Commission paid in stages, as the customer stays. For example: part at signature, part when the first settlement bill is paid, the rest at month twelve.

The Power of Prototype

Two interactive prototypes. Used before anything is built for real.

Most technology projects do not fail in development. They fail before it, in requirements nobody reads and features argued in the abstract. So each one ends in something you can open and use, not something you have to imagine: an interactive prototype on a live URL, following Volton’s brand guidelines on a design system we build for it.

You experience it. You do not read it.A live URL your team opens and uses, with the whole journey in front of them. Problems surface in hours, on the screen, not months later in the build.
It runs on dummy data.Nothing to integrate, nothing to break. The flows get settled before one live connection is built.
The holistic approach.The end-to-end experience of the consumer, with the core flows and scenarios covered, rather than one screen at a time.
What are the limitationsNo surprises here either. It is not a set of Figma screens, and it does not carry live integrations, production data, enterprise security or scale. Those arrive with the production build, with IT and the vendors, who own the infrastructure and are the ones who can integrate it safely and accurately. The prototype is what makes that build a straight line.
